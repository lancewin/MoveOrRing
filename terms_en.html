2. About the app

This app is a tool for keeping you on top of an activity goal: with your permission it reads activity data from the system Health app, decides whether you have reached the goal you set for the day, and reminds you by local notification at the frequency you choose. There is no sign-up and no login, and your activity data is stored only on your device.

This app is not a medical or health-advice tool and offers no diagnosis, treatment, or rehabilitation advice.

3. Health and safety notice

  • Please set a goal that suits your own condition. Do not exercise beyond what you can safely handle just to hit the number.
  • If you have a cardiovascular or joint condition, or you are pregnant or recovering from surgery, please use this app under a doctor's guidance.
  • If you feel chest pain, dizziness, or shortness of breath while exercising, stop immediately and seek medical help.
  • While driving, cycling, operating machinery, or in any situation that needs your attention, do not let checking your progress distract you.
  • Any exercise decision you make based on this app's numbers or reminders is taken at your own risk.

5. Platform limits (worth knowing before you rely on it)

The limits below come from iOS itself and are not something this app can work around. We state them plainly so you do not form unrealistic expectations:

  • The progress figure in a reminder is the reading taken at the moment it was scheduled: while the app is in the foreground it reschedules roughly every 10 seconds, so the number is fresh; once it is backgrounded or killed, the number stays at the last reschedule. Reminders scheduled ahead for future days carry no figure — they state the goal only.
  • If you force-quit the app entirely, iOS will not wake it just because a reminder is due; if iOS delivers new health data in the background (timing is up to the system) the app is woken once and reschedules. Either way the local notification still arrives on time, but its figure may be the one from the last refresh.
  • Cancelling the rest of the day's reminders once you have hit the goal requires the app to be running or woken by the system; if you have not opened it for a long time, you may receive one reminder that no longer means anything.
  • The home screen widget is refreshed by the system on its own budget (roughly every 15–30 minutes) — it is not real time.
  • Activity data depends on the system Health records. If the device did not carry it, access was not granted, or the system produced no samples, the app shows "can't read your data right now" — and this is never recorded as a missed day.
  • The app does not promise that a reminder will appear at an exact minute. Please do not make important arrangements depend entirely on its reminders.
